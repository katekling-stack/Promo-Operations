The problem today

Promo campaigns are trafficked by hand — dozens of placements per order across tiers, durations, formats, regions and platforms, each with precise targeting, ad units, geo, flight timing and exclusions. Coordinators rely on institutional knowledge, common sense, and scattered reference docs. That’s slow, error-prone, inconsistent, and hard to scale or onboard.

What changes

Today — manual

  • Dozens of settings keyed by hand per order
  • Targeting from judgment + manual lookups
  • Errors surface after campaigns go live
  • Output varies by who built it
  • Rules live in people’s heads

With the tool

  • Rule-complete draft generated in seconds
  • Targeting grounded in real inventory + history
  • Malformed orders blocked before push
  • Same rules every time — consistent & auditable
  • Best practice encoded once, in the system

Why it moves the needle

−75% errors Rule-complete by construction

The settings people mis-key today — site groups, ad units, exclusions, rating VGs, flight dates — are applied consistently and validated before push. Fewer live-campaign incidents, make-goods, and re-trafficking cycles.

−60% time Review, don’t rebuild

Significant manual setup becomes a reviewed draft in seconds. Coordinators shift from building placements to QA — absorbing more volume without adding headcount.

Accuracy Consistent & auditable

Every order follows the same rule set, mirrored from the live reference IOs. Deterministic and reviewable — and the tool never books on its own; a human activates every draft.

Smarter targeting Real data, not guesswork

Suggested Targeting proposes comp shows, Pluto channels, genres & categories — matched to our real FreeWheel inventory and what similar past campaigns actually ran. Huge for new titles and onboarding.

Illustrative: at ~1,000 orders/yr at ~45 min each, a 60% cut saves ~450 hours/year — before counting the error-rate savings, which avoid the costliest failures (live mis-targeting). Swap in our real volumes to finalize.
