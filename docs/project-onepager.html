Automating FreeWheel Order & Placement Setup

Turning a repetitive, manual, error-prone build process into a config-driven tool — so Campaign Managers spend their time on strategy and targeting, not data entry.

The problem

Setting a promo campaign live in FreeWheel means hand-building an Insertion Order plus ~14 placements — every tier, targeting set, frequency cap, geo, time zone, and self-exclusion typed in by hand, for every campaign, in every market.

  • It’s slow. The team can set 20+ campaigns live per day, each a lengthy manual build.
  • It’s error-prone. Manual re-entry across dozens of fields invites typos, missed exclusions, and inconsistent naming/targeting — plan to plan, order to order, market to market.
  • It’s the wrong use of expert time. Skilled Campaign Managers spend hours on mechanical setup instead of the work that actually moves the needle: building targeting, support plans, and thoughtful setup.

What we identified & built

The build is repetitive and rule-based — the same tiering, naming, caps, geo/time-zone, and self-exclusion logic applied over and over. That makes it a perfect fit for automation. We built Promo Ops: a config-driven tool that turns a simple campaign form into a fully-built, review-ready FreeWheel draft.

Every draft is built from one source of truth, so tiering, naming, frequency caps, geo, market time zone, and self-exclusions come out identical and correct every time. It runs one campaign or a whole batch at once, and never creates duplicates.

Proven — it’s working today

Promo Ops is built, tested, and validated live against FreeWheel production. It has created real NOT_BOOKED drafts end-to-end (14 placements each) across domestic and international markets, with frequency caps, market time zones, and self-exclusions all applied automatically. It’s in an interim self-serve rollout now (a form + Slack submission), running by hand on an Ad Ops workstation.
